📋 Universal Timetable Format Reference

Our analyzer works for any degree (B.A., BBA, BCA, B.Tech, B.Com, B.Sc) and any subjects. To ensure accurate extraction, your uploaded timetable must follow an academic tabular structure with period columns, day rows, and cell entries (subject, room, teacher):

1. Table Grid: Structured rows & columns (not a plain text list)
2. Period Columns: Times or Period numbers (Period 1 to 8)
3. Cells: Subject title, Teacher/Faculty name, Room/Lab
4. Indian College Notation: Theory/Lab brackets like Th(1-3) or (4-6) are automatically parsed
